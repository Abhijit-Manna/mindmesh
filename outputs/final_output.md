## Delivery Planner – Online Platform for Booking Home‑Healthcare Services  
**Maximum delivery horizon:** **3 months** (12 weeks)  

---

### 1. Delivery Overview  
| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Goal** | Build a **Minimum Viable Product (MVP)** that lets patients discover, book, pay for, and review home‑healthcare services; lets caregivers manage their schedules and earnings; and provides basic admin & support tooling. |
| **Scope** | End‑to‑end web‑based flow (patient portal + caregiver portal + admin console) with core functional blocks: user‑registration & verification, service catalogue, calendar‑based booking, payment processing, messaging, and reporting dashboards for admins/agencies. |
| **Timeline** | **3 months** – 4 weeks per major workstream (over‑lapped where possible). All
