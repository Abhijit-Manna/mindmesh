Delivery Plan: Secure Telehealth Platform

1. Delivery Overview

This plan outlines a 6-month delivery trajectory to launch a secure, compliant telehealth platform in the Azure Singapore region. The strategy prioritizes the use of Azure Managed Services (PaaS/SaaS) to accelerate development, focusing team efforts on the clinical business logic and regulatory compliance.

2. Business/MVP Scope and Priorities

  • High Priority (MVP):
    • Clinical Flow: AI Symptom Triage, Appointment Scheduling, Secure Video Consultations, EHR Read/Write.
    • Prescription: Digital prescription generation/dispatch.
    • Foundation: User Authentication (MFA), RBAC, Audit Logging.
  • Deferred (Future): Wearable integration, multilingual support, advanced predictive analytics, insurance billing, and mobile native apps.

3. Implementation Workstreams

5. Milestones

Milestone Target Month Exit Criteria
Environment Readiness Month 1 Azure Landing Zone, Entra ID, and CI/CD pipelines active.
Core API/Triage Alpha Month 3 Triage flow functional; CRUD on appointments.
Consultation Beta Month 4 Successful end-to-end video call; EHR read/write.
System Integration Month 5 Full workflow (Triage → Consult → Prescription) verified.
Go-Live Readiness Month 6 Security Audit pass, Load test (10k DAU) success, UAT sign-off.

6. Dependencies and Prerequisites

  • EHR Access: Immediate access to partner EHR documentation/API/Test environment.
  • Compliance: Confirmation of PDPA/HSA requirements for digital prescriptions.
  • Identity: Approval of authentication workflows (if Singpass is required).

7. Effort and Complexity Assessment

  • Complexity: High (Due to strict regulatory requirements and legacy EHR integration).
  • Effort: Significant; the 6-month timeline is tight. By utilizing Azure Managed Services (ACS, Entra ID), we avoid "reinventing the wheel" for video/auth, allowing the team to focus on the 60% of the effort residing in the EHR Gateway and clinical workflows.

8. Testing and Quality Activities

  • Automated: xUnit/Jasmine unit tests in the CI/CD pipeline.
  • Security: DAST/SAST scans; PEN-testing of the API Gateway and EHR Gateway.
  • Compliance: Validation of "immutable audit logs" and encryption-at-rest.

9. Integration Activities

  • EHR Gateway: Integration with legacy system via VPN/Private Link.
  • Azure Services: Connecting APIM to AKS; linking Entra ID to the SPA/API.
  • Notification: Linking ACS Email/SMS for appointment triggers.

10. Deployment and Release Activities

  • Deployment: Infrastructure as Code (Terraform) deployed to Singapore region.
  • Release: Blue/Green deployment strategy on AKS to ensure zero downtime.
  • Documentation: Finalize operational runbooks and compliance reports for stakeholders.

11. Delivery Risks

  • Regulatory: Misinterpretation of local health data handling laws.
  • EHR Integration: Unexpected legacy system downtime or protocol mismatch.
  • Performance: Latency in video/data under 10,000 DAU load.

12. Risk Mitigations

  • Regulatory: Engage a compliance consultant early in Month 1 for "privacy-by-design" validation.
  • EHR: Prototype the integration in Month 2 to identify data gaps early.
  • Performance: Use Azure Load Testing early (Month 4) to tune AKS scaling and Redis caching.

13. Future Evolution

  • Phase 2: Integration with national identity systems (Singpass) and wearable devices.
  • Phase 3: Machine Learning (Azure ML) integration for predictive triage and patient health analytics.
